The edit

Built to be watched,
not skipped.

I specialize in high-converting, on-brand short-form video for DTC women's brands. Every project starts with the hook — the first second decides whether someone stays or swipes — then gets built out with AI-generated footage and finished with hands-on editing so the pacing, captions and sound design actually feel like the brand.

My workflow blends Higgsfield, Veo 3.1, ChatGPT and Claude for fast, on-brief generation with CapCut Pro for the final polish — hooks, pattern interrupts, caption timing, and sound design that make a video feel native to the feed instead of made by AI.

Secret weapon

15 years of reading an audience.

Before video, I spent over a decade in full-cycle recruitment & customer service — sourcing, screening and matching people at scale, on a deadline, under KPIs. That's the same instinct behind every hook: know exactly who you're talking to, and don't waste their first second.
